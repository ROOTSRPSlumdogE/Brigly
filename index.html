Good to know

Questions.

Short answers. The demo has the long ones.

Is this a real product I can use today?+

Brigly is a working prototype. The demo runs fully in your browser with a fictional restaurant and six months of sample history. There is no sign-up, backend or billing yet.

How do crew and manager views fit together?+

Same shift, same data. When crew acknowledge a briefing, submit a list or request a swap, it lands on the manager’s Today screen for one-tap approval. Switch roles in the demo to see both sides.

Does it replace my POS?+

The POS module covers floor, orders, kitchen routing, payments, tips and the daily close, built on the same menu and floor plan as the rest of the workspace.

Does it work on phones?+

Yes. Phones get bottom navigation, bottom-sheet forms and day rosters. Desktop keeps the weekly planning grid and wide reports.

What about allergens?+

Recipes are marked as drafts until a manager verifies them. Only verified records count for service lookups and training, and allergy questions always point back to the kitchen record.
