QUESTIONS

Good to know.

How Brigly brings calm structure to daily restaurant operations.

How do the Team Member and Manager views work together?+

Staff and managers work from the same live shift context with role-tailored interfaces. When a team member acknowledges a briefing, submits an Opening or Closing checklist, requests a shift swap, or flags low stock, it appears immediately in the manager’s Service Command and Review Inbox for one-tap approval.

Can managers build schedules and customize opening & closing lists?+

Yes. In Manager View, you can assign shifts by department and station in the Schedule Studio, fill open weekend shifts, approve shift swaps, and manage both Opening and Closing station checklists across Bar, Dining Room, and Kitchen.

Is Brigly built for mobile phones on the floor?+

Yes. Team members get a fast, thumb-friendly mobile experience with bottom navigation for schedules, briefings, checklists, and menu lookup, while managers can run approvals and rosters from a phone, tablet, or laptop.

How does Brigly keep menu, wine, and allergen information reliable?+

Draft or imported recipes are clearly marked until verified by a manager. Only approved house records are marked authoritative for pre-shift training, service lookup, and allergy protocols.
