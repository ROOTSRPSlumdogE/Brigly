Welcome to Brigly. By using this website and the demo, you agree to the few points below.

The demo restaurant is made up

The Copper Room, its team, its briefings, its recipes and its orders are a sample restaurant. It exists to show how Brigly connects the kitchen, the floor, the bar and the office. Any likeness to a real place or person is a coincidence.

Check your own kitchen’s facts

The dishes, allergen notes and food-safety details in the demo are examples. A real restaurant must always check ingredients, allergens and safe temperatures against its own kitchen and its local rules.

It’s a preview

Brigly is still being built. Features can change, and the demo comes as it is, with no promise that it fits any particular business.
