Preview terms

This website demonstrates an early idea for Brigly. It does not provide a production restaurant management service.

Use sample information only

The Copper Room, its staff and its operating data are fictional examples. Scheduling, approvals, training progress, messages and supply requests simulate workflows. They do not create real commitments, notify people, verify competency or place orders.

Do not rely on the preview for service

Sample recipes, menu entries and procedures are not verified operational instructions or allergy guarantees. Use your restaurant's approved records and established safety procedures.

Availability and plans

Features, pricing, pilot scope and release timing have not been committed. There is no subscription, payment or service-level agreement. Demo data can be lost or reset; the preview provides no backup or account recovery.

Questions and future service

A business contact and the terms for any real pilot will be published before registration opens. See privacy in the preview for current data handling.
