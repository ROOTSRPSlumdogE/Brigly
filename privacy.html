Privacy & Data Handling

Brigly is designed around calm, privacy-first hospitality operations. This interactive website and workspace run locally in your browser with no external tracking cookies or third-party analytics.

Local workspace storage

When you interact with The Copper Room workspace—such as acknowledging a shift briefing, checking off opening tasks, posting in team channels, or switching between Team Member and Manager views—your changes are saved locally in your browser’s localStorage so your session stays connected across views.

You can reset the workspace to its default Friday evening state at any time using the Reset workspace button in the sidebar or footer.

Zero third-party trackers

All typography (Plus Jakarta Sans), icons, and assets are hosted locally with the site. No behavioral advertising or third-party tracking scripts are loaded.
