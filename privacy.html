Privacy in the preview

Brigly is an early product concept. Registration is closed, and this website has no active signup, payment or account form.

What stays in your browser

The interactive workspace saves sample actions in your browser's local storage: demo messages, briefing edits, acknowledgments, requests, task progress and role selection. They are not sent to a restaurant, a Brigly server or other users. Use fictional information only, especially on a shared device.

Select Reset demo in the workspace to replace your actions with the original sample data. You can also remove the site's storage in your browser settings. Without a reset, data remains until your browser clears it. If storage is unavailable, the preview works for the current page session only.

Website requests

The website code includes no analytics, advertising trackers or externally hosted fonts. Opening a hosted version sends ordinary web requests to its hosting provider, which may process IP addresses, request times and browser information to deliver and secure the site. The provider and its policies must be identified before public hosting is activated.

Before registration opens

We will publish the business operator, a working privacy contact, hosting and form providers, the purpose of collection, retention periods and any applicable rights before asking for personal information. This preview notice does not describe a future live restaurant service.
