Privacy

Your data stays with you.

Brigly is in preview. This website and the demo run in your own browser. There are no tracking cookies, no ad pixels and no third-party analytics.

What the demo saves

When you use the demo, for example to confirm a briefing, tick off a checklist, post in team chat or switch between the staff and manager views, your changes are saved in your browser’s local storage. That keeps your session in one piece as you move between screens. Nothing is sent to a server.

You can wipe it any time with the Reset workspace button in the demo’s sidebar or footer, or by clearing your browser data.

Nothing loaded from outside

Fonts, icons and images all come with the site. No outside scripts run on these pages.
